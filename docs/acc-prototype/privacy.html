Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

Your portfolio stays on your device

The numbers you enter about your homes, loans and savings are stored on the device you use. They are not uploaded, and your information is never sold.

What is kept online

To sign you in, the app keeps only: your name, your sign-in email, how you sign in (password or Google), your permissions, a history of changes to your account, and which notices you've read. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, and by Google if you choose Continue with Google. Supabase also keeps its own sign-in records, such as when you signed in, your IP address and your browser.

Google sign-in

If you continue with Google, the app receives only your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and uses them only to create and sign in to your account. It does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Email and texts

The app never sends you texts. The only email it sends is a temporary password, when you ask for one or ask the app's owner to send one. It comes from the owner's Gmail, and a copy of that email, with your address, stays in his sent mail. Other messages from the owner are shown inside the app.

No tracking

There are no ads, analytics or tracking scripts.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. That removes your sign-in and account details; your portfolio on your device is yours to keep or clear.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
